I’m Silas, 22, a technical founder, developer and student. My interests include systems architecture, local inference with open-weight models, homelab infrastructure, 3D printing and physical prototypes.

As a child, I sat on the floor with sheets of A3 and A2 paper, trying to translate brain functions into programmable systems. Only later could I place those questions within the history of AI research.

For me, personal development also means becoming capable of carrying complex projects through: understanding relationships, working with uncertainty and taking responsibility as a founder.

Also: espresso, with every step of the routine accounted for. And Anton <3
